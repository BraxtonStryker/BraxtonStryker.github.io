Study apps

Study smarter with your own course material.

Four free tools that run entirely in your browser. Practice with quizzes built from your lectures, drill the same questions as flashcards, find the right slide or textbook page in seconds, and write it all up in Markdown.

QuizQrusher

Practice quizzes

Turn your course material into multiple-choice practice. AI writes the questions as a CSV; QuizQrusher runs the quiz, grades it, and shows where to focus next.

  • Tutor mode, timer, and retakes of missed or flagged questions
  • Category trends and a follow-up prompt aimed at what you missed
  • Ready-made prompts for medicine, dentistry, pre-med and engineering, plus a Prompt Builder for any subject
Open QuizQrusher

InfoBase

Search slides & textbooks

Add your lecture slides and textbook as PDFs and search every page at once. Paste the key words of a question and jump straight to the slide or page that answers it.

  • Forgiving search: typos, plurals and exact "phrases"
  • A tab for each class, plus practice question sets linked to the slides that answer them
  • Works offline, or download a whole class as one file
Open InfoBase

CwikCard

Flashcards

Turn the same question CSV into flashcards and review them with spaced repetition: rate each card, and it comes back just before you'd forget it.

  • FSRS, SM-2 (classic Anki) or Leitner scheduling, plus practice rounds that leave your schedule alone
  • Front to back, back to front, or both
  • Keyboard shortcuts, swipes, undo, flag, bury and suspend
Open CwikCard

RunDown

Markdown word processor

Write notes and reports in Markdown and see them formatted as you go: each piece renders the moment you close it, and opens back up when you click into it.

  • Headings, lists, checklists, tables, code and LaTeX math
  • A formatting toolbar, shortcuts, find and replace, and an outline
  • Export to Markdown, a web page, PDF, or formatted text for Google Docs and Word
Open RunDown
Your files stay with you. All four apps work on your device. Nothing you add is uploaded, and each can be installed and used offline.
