Study apps

Study smarter with your own course material.

Two free tools that run entirely in your browser. Practice with quizzes built from your lectures, then find the right slide or textbook page in seconds.

Quiz Qrusher

Practice quizzes

Turn your course material into multiple-choice practice. AI writes the questions as a CSV; Quiz Qrusher runs the quiz, grades it, and shows where to focus next.

  • Tutor mode, timer, and retakes of missed or flagged questions
  • Category trends and a follow-up prompt aimed at what you missed
  • Prompt Builder for any subject
Open Quiz Qrusher

Class Base

Search slides & textbooks

Add your lecture slides and textbook as PDFs and search every page at once. Paste the key words of a question and jump straight to the slide or page that answers it.

  • Forgiving search: typos, plurals and exact "phrases"
  • A tab for each class, plus practice question sets linked to the slides that answer them
  • Works offline, or download a whole class as one file

Your files stay with you. Both apps work on your device. Nothing you add is uploaded, and both can be installed and used offline.
